03 / YOU DON'T HAVE TO FIGURE IT OUT ALONE

Let's get you unstuck.

What should I work on today?
  1. Check your email and texts for instructor directions.
  2. Open your assigned learning platform and look for assignments.
  3. Not sure what comes next? Email Ryan.
How do I sign in or reset a password?
  1. Open the platform your instructor assigned.
  2. Use the username and password provided for that platform.
  3. If offered, use its “Forgot password” link and follow the directions. Check your email for a reset message.
  4. Still stuck? Tell Ryan which platform you're using and what the screen says. Don't send your password.
How do I join an online class?
  1. Open HyFlexKY and find your class time and joining instructions.
  2. Open the class link a few minutes before it starts.
  3. Follow the meeting prompts. Allow microphone access if needed to speak.
  4. If you cannot join, email Ryan with the class name and the message on your screen.
How do I find a downloaded file?
  1. Tap the download link your instructor sent. Note the file name.
  2. Open your phone's Files app or file manager. Look in Downloads; the location can vary by phone and browser.
  3. Tap the file to open it. If it is missing, check your browser's downloads list.
  4. Ask Ryan for help if you cannot find or open it.
How do I email completed work?
  1. Open your email app and start a message to ryan@laureladulted.org.
  2. Put your name and the assignment title in the subject.
  3. Use the attachment or paperclip button to choose your file. For paper work, attach clear photos of each page with all writing visible.
  4. Check that the files or photos are attached, then send the email.

Work completed inside your learning platform is recorded there; you do not need to email it again.

How do I fill out a contact form?
  1. Tap each labeled box and type your information.
  2. Include your name, a way to reach you, and your question.
  3. Check your email address or phone number for mistakes.
  4. Tap “Send message” and wait for confirmation. If sending fails, keep your text and try again or email Ryan.
Missed a class or a few days?

Come back and take the next step. Check your messages, open your assigned work, and contact Ryan if you need help restarting. Ask your instructor about a missed HyFlex class recording or related work.

How can I use less mobile data?
  • Start with written directions and text resources.
  • Save video viewing for Wi-Fi when possible.
  • Download needed files while connected to Wi-Fi. Some learning activities still require internet access.
